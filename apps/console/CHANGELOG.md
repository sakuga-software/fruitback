# @fruitback/console

## 0.9.0

### Minor Changes

- d7238ca: The account keeps the look of the console its person chose in Settings › Appearance: their other browsers show it once they sign in, and another account in the same browser does not. `POST /console/me/appearance` takes a whole appearance (eight known values) or `null`, and `GET /console/me` answers it. A migration adds the column `appearance` to `user`, empty for every account.
- 2203826: The list of the connectors says the health of each one: how many of its deliveries did not arrive, how many notes went to it, and when its last send arrived. The worker keeps that moment on the connector (migration 0054): when an address takes a delivery, and when a tracker takes a note. The Connectors screen shows it in a « Last delivery » column (« 12 min ago », « None yet »), counts the notes in « Used by » (« 2 sites · 31 notes »), says « Healthy » for a tracker that its tracker does not refuse, and reads the list of the sends of an address only when some of them wait.
- fac5b21: A reply can be written from the panel of a note in the console: one line at its foot, « Reply, it posts to Linear too », and « Send ». It goes under the note in the name of the person signed in, and to the tracker of the note, by the same way as a reply from the page (`POST /console/workspaces/<id>/notes/<note>/replies`). Whoever reads the note in the console may reply: a member on any site, a guest on a site shared with them. A refusal keeps the text, and so does a move to another note or Escape.
- 5d2b289: A site chooses how long it keeps a note its visitor deleted: « Deleted notes », the fourth line of the card « Safeguards » in its tab « Access », 7, 30 or 90 days, 30 by default. Meanwhile the panel of the note says when it goes, and a member restores it: its pin shows on its page again. Then the worker erases it for good: a note it keeps goes whole, and of a note of a tracker it erases the words it kept.
- 87b59e4: A site can hold the notes of its visitors for a member: « Hold new visitor notes », the second line of the card « Safeguards » in its tab « Access », off by default. A note sent with no account then waits in the database, `POST /feedback` answers `202` with `held: true`, and the widget says « Sent for review » with no pin. A member finds the notes in « To approve », a new entry of the views in the bar, and approves one (it goes to its place, as the visitor's own) or rejects it. A note waits 30 days at most.
- 0dcf745: A site can keep every version of its notes: « Keep every version », the third line of the card « Safeguards » in its tab « Access », on by default. An edit of the text of a note keeps the text of before, and a deletion keeps the note as it was, with who did it and when. The members of the workspace read them in a section « History » of the panel of the note; a guest reads none. The widget tells the author, in the form of an edit, that the team keeps the text of before (`versionsKept` of a read). A note keeps 50 versions at most, and they go with the note.
- c4f773f: In the console, the notes of a workspace scroll with no end, in place of pages: the next notes are read as the end of the list comes near, and « Load more » under the list reads them too, for the keyboard and a screen reader, which hears how many came. The total stays on the screen. A filter, the search, a view or another workspace starts the list again from its first notes. A read of the next notes that fails says so under the list with « Try again », and keeps the notes already shown. The screen keeps 500 notes at most; a filter or the search finds the others. The notes of the instance keep their pages.
- a9718cc: A Claude Code routine whose `/fire` answers `404` (the routine or its API trigger was deleted) now needs attention, as `routine-gone`: the call is given up, the Connectors screen says « Needs attention » on its row, its panel says that claude.ai says the routine is gone and how to connect one again, and the box « Start an agent » of a site that calls it says why its calls stopped. The next call that starts the routine ends the attention.
- a6a09b4: The bar of the console shows its counts as soon as a workspace opens: the open notes beside « Notes », the sites beside « Sites », the sends that did not arrive in red beside « Connectors », and the tasks of the person beside « Assigned to me ». One read gives them, `GET /console/workspaces/<id>/rail`, when the workspace opens and at each change of workspace. A guest counts the sites shared with them and their notes, and never the sends of the connectors.
- a6a09b4: The page of a site says « Installed » once the widget was seen on it, with « Seen on the site 2 h ago », and its figures follow the mockup: « Open » says the age of the oldest open note, and « Delivered » the share of the notes of the last 30 days that arrived, with a link to the ones that did not. The worker writes when the widget last read a page of a site, by the script or by the extension, at most once an hour: one date per site and per way, in two new columns of `sites` (migration `0055_sites_seen`), and nothing else.
- 814d8c1: Sends again, in one press, the notes of a site that did not arrive (FRU-717). On the Sites screen, the red arrow of the column « Sent to » is a button for an owner or an admin: `POST /console/workspaces/<id>/sites/<site>/retry` makes the deliveries of its notes due now, 100 at most, and answers how many leave and how many are left. The loop of the worker sends them, so a second press sends nothing twice. A member keeps the link to the notes of the site.
- 3dd070e: A task written by hand may have no site: it belongs to the whole workspace. In the console, « New
  task » offers « No site: the whole workspace » to a member, and a workspace with no site yet can
  write tasks too. Such a task takes the built-in statuses, and every field of a task. Every member
  reads and changes it as a task of a site; a guest neither sees nor writes one, and an agent key, which
  reads the sites it names, does not read it. It stays when a site is removed, and goes with its
  workspace. `POST /console/workspaces/<id>/notes` takes no `site` for it.
- a6a09b4: The bar of the console has the three views of its mockup: « New this week », « Assigned to me » and « Not delivered », each with its count, the last one in red when notes did not arrive. « Filter » on the Notes screen offers the same two filters, « Left: This week » and « Delivery: Not delivered », with their chips. The list of the notes takes `since`, an instant in milliseconds, and `undelivered=1`, which a guest is refused, and the read of the bar counts both.
- 71168b8: A person saves the filters of the Notes screen as a view of their own. « Save view » in the bar of filters names the filters that are on, and the view shows in the bar under « New this week », « Assigned to me » and « Not delivered », where its menu renames it or deletes it. The worker keeps the views in a table of their own, beside the membership of the person, so a view follows its person from one browser to another, and nobody else reads it: `GET` and `POST /console/workspaces/<id>/views`, `POST` and `DELETE …/views/<view>`. A person keeps only filters they can read, and 20 views in a workspace at most.
- cdc234d: A site limits the notes a visitor with no account leaves in an hour: the line « Notes per visitor » of the new card « Safeguards » in its tab « Access », « No limit », 5, 10, 20 or 50, and 10 by default. The worker counts them by the key of the browser and by the address, on the site, before anything is written, and answers `429 visitor-limit` over it. The widget then says « Send it again later » and keeps the text. A member and a page that signs its reporter are not counted. The migration gives 10 to every site, those of before included.

- 42be906: On the notes of a workspace, a chunk read as the list scrolls draws its own rows, and no longer the whole list again. At 500 notes, the page stayed busy about 0.4 s for each chunk; now about 0.08 s.
- 50c0e42: In the console, the list of the notes draws faster as it grows: « Loading… » and what a screen reader hears at the end of the list no longer draw every note again, and the day of each note is written with a format made once. At 450 notes a press of « Load more » took half the time.
