B · The settings of a Linear team

  • A drawer on the right, over the tab. It first says that the settings are shared by every site that sends to this place (decision 1).
  • « Status of arrival » lists the states of the team, read from Linear, each with the stage it maps to. Empty: the default state of Linear, as today.
  • Assignee, labels, priority and milestone are read from the tracker: the list the console offers is the list the worker accepts.
  • The capture: on or off, the element or the visible window. A default of the composer of the extension (decision 2).
  • The template: a title and a description with variables in double braces, no logic. The preview fills them with a note of example, and says that the block of the note follows, always.
  • The details: one box per line of the description of today. An unticked box removes the line, never the block.

Decisions of the board (fru-specs, 2026-11-28)

The orchestrator decided where the settings live and what a template touches. The decisions of the framing are in worker.md.

  1. Where. A drawer opened from the row of a place, in the tab « Destinations » of a site, and from the panel of the connector. The settings are of the place, in connectors.settings, so the drawer says that every site that sends there shares them.
  2. Who. An owner or an admin (manage-workspace), as the settings of a Linear connector today.
  3. Only what the tool takes. A setting the tool cannot take is one line that says so (GitHub: no status, no file), or nothing (Slack: no assignee). The worker refuses it on save.
  4. The block of the note never changes. The preview says it under the template, and the details say it under their boxes.
  5. A variable that does not exist is refused on save, with its name and the one to use. No logic in a template.
  6. A refused setting does not lose the note. The issue arrives without it, and the place says « Action required » until a save.
  7. One « Save » for the drawer. Everything of the place is written in one request; a change applies to the notes that come after it.
