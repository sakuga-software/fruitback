The statuses of a site (FRU-371)

Board of the console screen that lists, adds, edits, orders and deletes the statuses of one site (FRU-352, docs/decisions/statuses.md). Drawn on the tokens of the console (design/README.md) and the five status colours of FRU-334, with the stage names of #486 (new, todo, progress, done, closed). Frames at 1440 px are in English, frames at 390 px in French; every sentence of both is in the table at the end.

Generated by tools/build.mjs: edit the script, not this file. The ratios are computed with a copy of packages/shared/src/contrast.ts. A frame is a picture: its buttons do nothing.

Open questions, with the recommendation of the board

  1. Where the screen lives. Recommended: its own page, /w/:workspace/sites/:site/statuses, from a new item « Statuses » of the menu of a site row (owner and admin, manage-sites). A panel under the row would sit in a table of 860 px at the least, too narrow for an editor and its preview.
  2. One write per status, or one « Save » for the page (the sketch of statuses.md draws one). Recommended: one per status, as the API writes one (POST …/statuses); the order and the switch write at once. A page-wide « Save » would be several writes that can half fail.
  3. How many notes a status holds. GET …/statuses gives no count; only 409 status-in-use does. Recommended for the first version: « Delete » sends no moveTo; a 200 was an empty status, a 409 opens the step « Move them to » with its count (frame G), so frame F and G are the two answers of one press.   4. The word for a stage. The console never showed one: it called a stage « Status ». Recommended: « Stage » in English, « Phase » in French (« Étape » is already the step of the setup).
  5. « Hidden » (the column hidden): it only changes what the status select of a note offers (FRU-367). Recommended: not drawn on this screen until that select exists.
  6. « Use Linear's colours » has no route yet (FRU-368), and GET /connectors/<id>/states does not read color. Recommended: the Linear part of the screen ships after FRU-368; the own store and GitHub parts do not wait for it.
  7. A text under 4.5:1. Recommended: « Save » stays enabled, a press sends nothing and moves focus to the text field, which points to the message (frame C). A disabled button says nothing to a screen reader.
  8. The table of FRU-102 on the page of the Linear connector. Recommended: removed in the same PR, replaced by a link to the statuses of each site that sends there: two places to set one stage is one too many.
  9. The status select in the list of notes, which statuses.md gives to FRU-371 too. Recommended: its own PR after FRU-367 (the route that changes the status of a note), not in this screen.
  10. « Reset » asks nothing. Recommended: no confirmation; what it removes is a name and a colour, typed again in seconds, and the notes do not move.
  11. « Colour » or « Color ». The console has no word of either yet; the ticket and statuses.md write « colour ». Recommended: « colour ».
