Decisions of the board (fru-agents, 2026-10-28)

The orchestrator asked the agent to decide the choices of the screen itself and to write them down. Each one can be changed by Mathieu.

  1. Where the screen lives. A page of its own, /w/:workspace/settings/agents, with the crumbs « Workspace settings › Agents », reached from a section « Agents » of the settings. Not a section of the rail: the rail holds the five of the mockup, and only the owner and the admins may make a key (decision 5 of agents.md). A section inside the settings page would put a list, a form and a log under the logo.
  2. The key is shown once, in a card above the list, until « I kept it ». It is held in the state of the screen only, never in a storage of the browser. The card says it is the only time, as the REST connector says it of its secret.
  3. The line for Claude Code holds the key and the address of this worker, as docs/agents.md writes it: one copy connects. A second button copies the key alone, for a runner's secrets. The link goes to the part of the guide for an agent that runs elsewhere.
  4. No edit of a key. The worker has no route for it, and a key that changes what it reaches under a running agent is a surprise. To change its sites or its rights: make another, give it to the agent, revoke the first.
  5. The end. A number of days (90 by default, 1 to 365) and the day it gives, written as it is typed. No « never »: decision 8.
  6. The warning 7 days before is said in words in the row (« Ends in 5 days », the strong accent and a triangle: the colour is never alone) and in one line above the list, which says what to do. No new token: the console has no amber.
  7. An ended key stays listed, with « Ended » and its day, since the worker keeps it; « Revoke » removes it.
  8. « Only the notes of known people » is asked for every key, ticked, even when none of its sites is read by everyone today: who reads a site can change after the key is made.
  9. The writes unfold under their row, read when they open. This is a list of rows, not the grid of cards where FRU-433 refused a panel between two rows. The note is named by its id, with no link: the console has no page of one note, and the id is what get_feedback takes.
  10. The last use is the one the worker keeps to the minute (PR of the worker of FRU-462), shown with ago() of the console, and « Never used » for a key no agent used.
  11. Who made a key is not shown. The worker answers an account id; the name would need the members, a second read, for a column the owner and the admins rarely need. Left for later.
  12. A member or a guest reads the section of the settings with a sentence and no link; the page says the same sentence and asks nothing of the worker.
