Decisions of the board (fru-tasks-bulk, 2026-11-24)

The orchestrator decides the open choices of the tasks module; the agent wrote these down, and each one can be changed by Mathieu.

  1. What can be selected. A task Fruitback keeps: a note with the fields of a task. A note of a tracker changes in its tracker, and has no box.
  2. The whole view is the page shown. Cmd or Ctrl and A, and the box of the head, select the tasks the list shows: one page of 25 at most, under the filters. A selection of every page would need the worker to change notes the screen never read; the bar offers the rest of the page, not of the list.
  3. The keys. Cmd or Ctrl and A selects the page when the focus is not in a field of text, a menu or a dialog: there, the key keeps its own work. Escape clears the selection when no menu, dialog, panel or question of the screen takes it first.
  4. A range. Shift and a click on a box, or on the row or the card out of its links and buttons, gives every task between the box pressed before and this one, in the order of the screen (the groups of the table, the columns of the board), the state of the one pressed.
  5. One change, one call, one transaction. POST …/tasks takes the notes and the change; every note is checked with the rights of a change of one task, and one refusal changes nothing. Before, the console had no gesture for several notes: a change of 25 tasks was 25 calls and 25 reads of the list, one after the other.
  6. Labels add and take off. A label every selected task has is checked; choosing it takes it off all of them, choosing another puts it on all of them. The other labels of each task stay.
  7. What a person cannot change stays in the selection. The bar says how many such tasks it holds, and the change goes to the others only: FRU-658 copies a selection, and a copy needs no right to change.
  8. The menu of FRU-654 on a selection is the same code, with the values checked when every task has them. Move up, Move down and Copy the link are for one note.
  9. The selection stays after a change, as long as its tasks are on the screen: a task archived, deleted or filtered out leaves it.
  10. Announced. The count of the selection, and the end of a change, are said by a line of status of the bar.
