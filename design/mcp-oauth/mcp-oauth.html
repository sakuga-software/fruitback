A · The question: an owner or an admin approves claude.ai

  • A page of the console outside a workspace, at /authorize/<request>, as « Connect this browser » (FRU-240): the worker sent the browser here from /oauth/authorize, once the client, its redirect_uri and its PKCE challenge were checked.
  • Who asks is said by the host of its redirect_uri, in large: claude.ai. The name the client gave itself at its registration is drawn small and said to be its own words: anybody can register a client named « Claude ».
  • The choices are those of a key (FRU-461): one workspace, its sites, Read or Read and write, the days, « Only the notes of a known person » on by default. The workspaces listed are those where the person is an owner or an admin (decision 5 of agents.md).
  • « Allow » makes the connection: an agent key with no secret, tied to the client. The browser goes back to the redirect_uri with a code of one use, the state, and iss.

Decisions of the board (fru-mcp-oauth, 2026-10-28)

The orchestrator asked the agent to decide the choices itself and to write them down. Each one can be changed by Mathieu. The decisions of the worker are in docs/decisions/agents.md and SECURITY.md.

  1. Where. A page outside a workspace, /authorize/<request>, as /connect/<request>: the person may not have chosen a workspace yet, and the page chooses one.
  2. Who asks is the host of its redirect_uri, the one thing a client cannot choose freely: the code goes there. Its name is its own words, drawn small.
  3. The choices of a key, with the same defaults: 90 days, « Only the notes of a known person » on. One workspace per connection: to reach two, connect twice.
  4. Never more than asked. A client that asked read is not offered « Read and write ». A client that asked nothing is offered both, Read checked.
  5. Who approves: an owner or an admin (manage-workspace), as for a key. The others read why, and can deny.
  6. In « Agents », a connection is a row of the list with a chip, no secret, and the same « Writes » and « Revoke ». A new list would be a second place to look for what reads the notes.
  7. Deny sends the client back with error=access_denied; closing the tab leaves the request to end in 10 minutes.
