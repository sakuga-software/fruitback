Nobody leaves the page. Nobody learns a new tool.

  1. Point

    Click what bothers you

    Hover the page, or move with the arrow keys and press Enter. The reporter never has to describe where the problem is.

  2. Send

    A note becomes an issue

    In the tracker your team already runs, or in your workspace, with the selector, the component and the file, and a picture of the element when the site offers one.

  3. Follow up

    The pin follows the issue

    When the client comes back, their pins are still there, coloured by the issue's status. Replies from the team show under each one.

  • Newjust sent
  • To doaccepted by the team
  • In progressbeing fixed
  • Donefixed
  • Closednot kept

The three modes, side by side

Self-hosted, no service of ours holds your feedback.

One compose file starts three containers: the worker, its PostgreSQL and the console. The tracker's API key stays on your server: it cannot ship in client-side JavaScript.

  • Notes in your PostgreSQL, or issues in Linear or GitHub Issues
  • Each site read by everyone, or by the members of its workspace only
  • Images for amd64 and arm64

From an empty folder to a first pin, in seven steps

Two files, five lines of .env, one command

$ curl -fsSLO https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sakuga-software/fruitback/main/docker-compose.yml
$ curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sakuga-software/fruitback/main/.env.example -o .env
# fill the five lines .env names
$ docker compose up -d --wait

Then one tag on the site, which the console gives you:

<script src="https://cdn.acme.dev/fruitback.iife.js"
  data-fruitback-endpoint="https://api.feedback.acme.dev"
  data-fruitback-client="site_…" defer></script>
