# Leave a note

A team asked for your feedback on their site. This page shows how to point at what should change, and
how to follow it. It takes two minutes. The full reference is
[Review with the extension](reviewing.md).

## 1. Find the button

Open the site. Look for « Leave a note » in a corner of the page.

- If you see it, go on to step 2. You need nothing to install. On a public site, you need no account.
- If it reads « Notes from members only », this site takes notes from its members only. Ask the team
  to invite you, then sign in with the email the invitation was sent to.
- If there is no button, the team uses the browser extension. Add it to
  [Chrome or Edge](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/fruitback/jjjemdloknmggncbickofjgbodplekoa)
  or to [Firefox](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/fruitback/). Sign in in the tab that
  opens, with the email the invitation was sent to. Then open the site, click the Fruitback icon in
  the toolbar, and press « Turn on ». The button appears.
- On Opera, a page of search results, Google included, shows no button until you allow it. Open
  `opera://extensions`, press « Details » under Fruitback, turn on « Allow access to search page
  results », then reload the page.

## 2. Point, write, send

1. Press « Leave a note ».
2. Click the part of the page your note is about: a button, a title, a price. On a phone, tap it.
   The Esc key stops without a note.
3. Write what should change in « Your note ». Say what you expected to see.
4. Press « Send ».

A pin stays on that part of the page. The team gets your note in their tracker, with the page, the size
of your screen and the element you chose. You need not describe where it is.

If the field ends with a badge « Guest », press it to add your name. Without a name, the note is
signed « Guest ». Signed in, you sign with your account.

## 3. Follow it

Come back to the page later. Your pins are still there, and the colour of each one tells where it is:

| Word        | What it means                   |
| ----------- | ------------------------------- |
| New         | The team has not read it yet.   |
| To do       | The team plans to do it.        |
| In progress | Somebody works on it.           |
| Done        | The team says it is fixed.      |
| Closed      | The team closed it with no fix. |

Click a pin to read the replies of the team, and to answer them.

When the team marks your note « Done », its thread asks « Does it look right to you? ». Press
« Looks fixed », or « Still wrong » and say what is still wrong: the note opens again.
