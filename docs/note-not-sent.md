# A note that did not go

The widget said « This note could not reach the team ». **Your note is not lost.** This page says
why it happens, and what to do.

[Lire en français](note-not-sent.fr.md)

## If you wrote the note

The site sends its notes to a place in a tracker, such as a project in Linear. That place no longer
exists, or it moved. Only the team of the site can choose another one.

1. Do not close the note. Its text stays in the field of the widget, in this browser.
2. Tell the person who gave you the link to this site. Say that the notes cannot reach the team.
3. When they say that it works again, open the note and send it again.

## If you are in the team of the site

A member of the workspace sees « Fix it in the console » in the widget, with a link to the page of
the site. To fix it:

1. Open the console, then **Sites**, then the site.
2. Under « Where the notes go », the place says « Needs attention ».
3. Add the right team or project, press « Make it the default » on it, then « Remove » on the old place.

The usual cause: in Linear, the project of the site moved to another team. The site keeps the old
team, and Linear refuses a project that is not in that team. Choose the team that has the project
now.

Only an owner or an admin of the workspace can change the place of a site.
