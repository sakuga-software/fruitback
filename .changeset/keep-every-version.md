A site can keep every version of its notes: « Keep every version », the third line of the card « Safeguards » in its tab « Access », on by default. An edit of the text of a note keeps the text of before, and a deletion keeps the note as it was, with who did it and when. The members of the workspace read them in a section « History » of the panel of the note; a guest reads none. The widget tells the author, in the form of an edit, that the team keeps the text of before (`versionsKept` of a read). A note keeps 50 versions at most, and they go with the note.
