The catalogue of the Connectors screen counts the connections of each kind: a green disc on the corner of its logo says how many workspaces of Linear or addresses are connected. « Add » takes the place of « Connected » and « + », and does what « Connect » does. A screen reader hears « Add another Linear (2 connected) ». The words of the tile have more room. A person who cannot add one still reads « Connected », and hears the count. The table already had a row for each connection, told apart by its account.
