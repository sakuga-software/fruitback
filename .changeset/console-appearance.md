Settings › Appearance: each person picks the look of their console. Five themes (Slate, the default, Snow, Zinc, Midnight and Graphite), the mode (Light, Dark or System), the tint of the sidebar, the accent, the density of the rows (32, 36 or 40 px), the radius (0, 4, 6 or 8 px) and the size of the text (12, 13 or 14 px), with « Reset ». A choice shows at once and stays in this browser, with no flash of the default theme at the next visit. Every theme, tint and accent keeps the contrasts of WCAG AA.
