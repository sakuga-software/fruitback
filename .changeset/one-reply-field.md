The field of a reply is the same everywhere: two lines, Enter sends, Shift+Enter goes to the next line, and the text is kept. The thread of the widget opens its reply on two lines; the side panel of the extension sends on Enter, and keeps what was typed in a reply after « Cancel », a closed row or another tab, until the reply goes.
