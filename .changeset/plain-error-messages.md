The messages of a note, a reply or an edit that did not go say what to do next, with no dash: « Not sent. Your text is still here. Try again. » in place of « Did not go through — your text is kept, try again ». A file whose bytes do not match its type, or that did not upload, says how to fix it; the foot of the composer reads « Creates a new issue in {destination} »; and « Pick again » is « Choose another element ». A host that overrides these keys through `init({ messages })` keeps its own words.
