The thread of a note follows the mock-up of 2026-11-25: 360 px wide with a corner of 10 px, a head of 40 px with the status in a chip (the round of its stage and its word), the ticket with the mark of its tracker and the sign of a new tab, the menu and the cross. The note and its replies are one conversation, each with the initial of who wrote, the name, the age and the text. « Technical details » folds the selector, the component and the file under the conversation: the option `showSelectors` opens it. The field to reply is one line with « Send » inside it. The dock shows the counts of the page by status between the list and the settings.
