A workspace can connect ClickUp: by consent at ClickUp on a server whose operator sets `FRUITBACK_CLICKUP_OAUTH`, or by a personal token anywhere. Each note becomes a task of the list a site chooses, with its files attached, and its pin takes the stage of the status of the task. Fruitback keeps the token encrypted, and says « Needs attention » when ClickUp refuses it.
