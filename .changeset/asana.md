A workspace can connect Asana: by consent at Asana on a server whose operator sets `FRUITBACK_ASANA_OAUTH`, or by a personal access token anywhere. Each note becomes a task of the project a site chooses, with its files attached, and its pin takes the stage of its completion or of its section. Fruitback keeps the tokens encrypted, revokes a consent when its connector goes, and says « Needs attention » when Asana refuses it.
