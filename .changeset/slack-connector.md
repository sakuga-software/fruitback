A workspace can post its notes in a Slack channel, by an Incoming Webhook. Each verified note, or each note if the connector says so, is posted with its link, its element and its capture, while the notes stay in Fruitback. The webhook is kept encrypted, and the panel of the connector lists the notes that did not reach Slack.
