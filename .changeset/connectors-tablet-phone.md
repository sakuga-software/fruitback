The Connectors screen fits a tablet and a phone. Where the table is narrower than 900 px, « Used by » goes, and « Retry » and « … » stay inside the frame. On a phone each connector is a card: its logo, its name and « … », its account, then its health with its sites and its last delivery on one line, and « Retry » on a line of its own. No tile of the catalogue is narrower than 260 px, so there are two in a row on a tablet. On a phone the search takes the whole width in 16 px, and the groups stay on one line. With a finger, « … », « Retry » and the buttons of a tile take a press in 44 px.
