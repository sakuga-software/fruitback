My account and the settings of the workspace share one shell: a bar that says « Settings », a navigation of 220 px with an icon on each entry, in two groups (Account: Profile, Sessions; Workspace: General, AI, Agents, Members, Connectors), and the page beside it. My account is the page « Profile »: four cards in two columns from 1280 px, one column under it. The profile, the last three browsers where you are signed in (« See all » opens the new page « Sessions »), the addresses and how you sign in. Each row of a card has an icon. The rare actions are in the menu of a row or in a dialog: « Make primary » and « Remove » of an address, « Disconnect » of a browser, « Add » an address, « Sign out everywhere ». « Sign out » is the action of the head of the profile. The addresses of the pages do not change.
