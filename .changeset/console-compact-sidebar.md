The console has a compact bar in place of the dark rail: 232 px on the slate of the sidebar, the workspace and a search of the notes at its top, the sections Notes, Sites, Connectors, Members and Settings in 28 px entries, and a fixed foot with « What's new » (the releases, with a dot until this version is seen), the extension (a green dot and the browser when it is connected), « Help and docs », and the person, whose menu opens « My account » and signs out. « My account » leaves the list of the sections. Under 1024 px the bar is folded above the screen, and « Menu » unfolds it. The administration of the instance has the same bar.
