Several tasks that Fruitback keeps can be selected at once, by their boxes, with Shift and a click for a range, or with Cmd or Ctrl and A for the page. A bar under the list changes their status, assignee, priority, labels or due day, archives them or deletes them, in one call that changes all of them or none; the menu of a selected task acts on the whole selection.
