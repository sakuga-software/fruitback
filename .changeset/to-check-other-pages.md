« To check » in the side panel lists the notes to check of the other pages of the site, the ones the badge counts, under « On other pages ». Each has « Looks fixed », « Still wrong » and « Open on its page », and its verdict goes from the panel. Before, the badge could count notes that no list showed. `GET /session/notes` takes `check=1`: the notes of the reader that wait for their verdict.
