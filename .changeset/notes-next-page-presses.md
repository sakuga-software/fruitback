In the console, each press of « Next page » counts, even when it comes while the next page is read: it goes on from the page asked last, not from the page on the screen. Two quick presses now go two pages on instead of one, and « Previous page » takes back a press that waits. The notes of a workspace and the notes of the instance both read their pages this way. Nothing changes on the screen: no button is turned off while a page is read.
