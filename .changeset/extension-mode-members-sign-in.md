Extension mode on a site of Fruitback Cloud that its members read: the popup says it in plain words and offers what fixes it. Signed out, « Only the members of this site see its notes and leave one here » with « Sign in to Fruitback ». Signed in as a member, « Turn on with my account » turns the site of the workspace on in place of the extension-mode rule, and the widget reads and writes with the account. Signed in with an account that is not a member, the popup says to ask the team of the site for an invitation. Extension mode stays available on the Cloud, and a server run by somebody else keeps its sentences.
