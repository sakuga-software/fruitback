The page of a site is in tabs: Overview, Install, Destinations, Statuses, Access and Advanced, each at an address of its own, under a head with « Open site » and « See its N notes ». « Overview » shows three figures (the notes and their statuses, the new ones, the share delivered), the settings of the site in rows with « Change », where its notes go with the logo of each tracker and a menu, and a card « Install » on the right with the script on a dark block and « Copy ». The card says « Not seen yet » until a note comes through the script or the extension: the worker records no visit of the script yet. A link to a section of the page of before opens its tab.
