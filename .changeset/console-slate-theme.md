The console takes the slate theme of the redesign: slate greys on a white page, one radius of 6 px for every control (8 px for a card, 10 px for a modal), three heights of a control (24, 28 and 32 px), and Inter at 13 px, served by the console. Each token is a CSS variable, so a setting of the appearance can change it at once. The extension, the widget and the docs do not change.
