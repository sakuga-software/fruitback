A workspace can connect GitLab, on gitlab.com or on a self-managed GitLab that the internet reaches, by a project access token (Reporter, scope `api`). Each note becomes an issue of the project a site chooses, with its labels and its files, and its pin takes the stage of the state of the issue. The token is kept encrypted, every call to a self-managed GitLab is guarded against an internal address, and the connector says « Needs attention » when GitLab refuses the token or a project.
