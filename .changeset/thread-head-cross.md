The bubble of a note keeps its close button at the top on the right: the head is two columns, and the state and the link wrap in the first. The age of the status leaves the head for the title of the status word. The selector and the source show only with `showSelectors`, as discreet lines of mono, and a note with no reply shows no « Replies » section.
