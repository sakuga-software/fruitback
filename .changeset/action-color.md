The widget takes `actionColor` at `init`, and `setActionColor` later: the colour of « Send » in the composer, refused under 4.5:1 with white text. The extension has an « Appearance » page where a person chooses the colour of the note button (Fruitback red, Deep red, Orange, Ink, or their own): it paints « Leave a note » in the popup and the side panel, and « Send » of the widget it mounts, for that person only.
