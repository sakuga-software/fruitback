The bubble of a note on the page moves by a handle at the start of its head: by a pointer, by the arrow keys (16 px, 48 px with Shift), and back to its place with Home or a double click. A dashed line joins it to its pin while it is away, and it follows its pin when the page scrolls. The move is forgotten when the bubble closes.
