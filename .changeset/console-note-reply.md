A reply can be written from the panel of a note in the console: one line at its foot, « Reply, it posts to Linear too », and « Send ». It goes under the note in the name of the person signed in, and to the tracker of the note, by the same way as a reply from the page (`POST /console/workspaces/<id>/notes/<note>/replies`). Whoever reads the note in the console may reply: a member on any site, a guest on a site shared with them. A refusal keeps the text, and so does a move to another note or Escape.
