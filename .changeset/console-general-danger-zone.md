The page « General » of the settings of a workspace is drawn again in the shell of the settings: a card « Identity », with a row for the logo, the name and the address of the workspace, and « Save » in the head of the page, disabled until the name changes. The deletion of the workspace moves to a « Danger zone » with a red border, for the owner alone: « Delete… » opens a dialog that deletes only once the name of the workspace is typed in it.
