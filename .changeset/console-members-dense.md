Members is one dense table: a line of 36 px per person with their address, their role, their sites, the day they were last active and the day they joined, and « … » to remove them. An owner or an admin changes a role in its row; a guest made a member asks twice, as they then read every site. The invitations that wait are a tab of their own, with their counts and the search. « Invite people » takes several addresses at once, offers the role as three cards that say what each one does, and « Send N invites » counts the valid addresses.
