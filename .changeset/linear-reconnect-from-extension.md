An owner or an admin reconnects a Linear that Linear ended from the popup itself: « Reconnect Linear » under « Sent to » opens the consent at Linear in a new tab, and Linear brings the browser back to the panel of the connector in the console, with its sites and its settings. A worker that refuses, or a tracker other than Linear, still opens the connector in the console.
