'@fruitback/console': minor

In the console, the notes of a workspace scroll with no end, in place of pages: the next notes are read as the end of the list comes near, and « Load more » under the list reads them too, for the keyboard and a screen reader, which hears how many came. The total stays on the screen. A filter, the search, a view or another workspace starts the list again from its first notes. A read of the next notes that fails says so under the list with « Try again », and keeps the notes already shown. The screen keeps 500 notes at most; a filter or the search finds the others. The notes of the instance keep their pages.
