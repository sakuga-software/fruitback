A site chooses how long it keeps a note its visitor deleted: « Deleted notes », the fourth line of the card « Safeguards » in its tab « Access », 7, 30 or 90 days, 30 by default. Meanwhile the panel of the note says when it goes, and a member restores it: its pin shows on its page again. Then the worker erases it for good: a note it keeps goes whole, and of a note of a tracker it erases the words it kept.
