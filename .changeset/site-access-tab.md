The page of a site has a tab « Access »: who reads the notes and who leaves them, as four levels (Closed, Read only, Contribute, and Collaborate to come), and a table of what a visitor may do. A visitor's right to change the text of their own note and to take it back is a setting of each site, `never`, `while-open` or `always`, kept by the worker in two new columns. « Who can leave a note » now decides on a site that everyone reads too: « Read only » is read by anyone and written by its members. The migration gives every site that everyone read « Anyone on the page », and every site of before `never` for both rights, so no site changes.
