A key typed in a field of the widget no longer reaches the shortcuts of the page. On the console, each « c » of a note opened « New task » and was missing from the note. The keys of the widget (Escape, Cmd or Ctrl and Enter, a paste, an IME) work as before, and while a field of the page has focus, the capture mode and the thread leave its keys to it.
