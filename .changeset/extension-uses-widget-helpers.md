`@fruitback/widget` exports `RELATIVE_UNITS`, the units its relative dates count in, from a module of its own. The extension counts the ages of its side panel with them, and relays files with the widget's `base64OfBytes` and `bytesOfBase64` in place of its copies. Nothing it shows or sends changes.
