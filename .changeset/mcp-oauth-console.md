« Connect an agent »: the page of the console where an owner or an admin answers an app that connects to `/mcp` with OAuth, as a connector of claude.ai. It names the app by the host its answer goes to, and asks for the workspace, the sites, the rights (never more than asked), the days and « Only the notes of known people ». A connection is listed in « Agents » with the chip « OAuth · host », and revoked there.
