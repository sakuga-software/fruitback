The Sites screen is a dense table, one line of 36 px per site: the site and a link to it, its notes by status and how many, its new notes in red, where they go with the logos of the trackers (and the icon of a retry in red when an address did not take some), who sees them, the last note and the menu. A press on a row opens the page of its site. « Sites » and « Add a site » are in a head of 48 px, and the bar under it holds the search by address, which « / » reaches, and the colours of the statuses. A band under the table names the sites that keep their notes in Fruitback, with « Connect a tracker ». The columns go by the width of the table: at 390 px a row keeps the site, its new notes and its menu.
