The list of the notes has three tabs that follow the loop, in the widget and in the side panel of the extension: « To check », « Open » and « Done ». « To check » holds the notes of the reader that the team marked done and nobody confirmed; it replaces the block « Ready to check » above the tabs. The list opens on « To check » while a note waits there. In the widget, the search, the scope and the chips move behind « Search and options », the magnifier of the head, as in the side panel. `noteTab` and `firstTab` of `@fruitback/shared` sort a note into its tab.
