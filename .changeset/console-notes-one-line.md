The Notes list is one line of 36 px per note, as dense as Linear: twelve notes show at 1440 × 900 where five did. Each status is a group whose head folds it, which the console remembers for each person, and « + » there writes a new task in that status. A line holds the status, the text at 500 on one line, the path of the page, the site, where the note went (« Failed » in red with a button that sends it again), who left it (a dashed round for a visitor) and its age. A narrow list drops the columns that have no room, in place of the cards. The keys j and k move between the notes, and Enter opens one.
