The bar of the console has the three views of its mockup: « New this week », « Assigned to me » and « Not delivered », each with its count, the last one in red when notes did not arrive. « Filter » on the Notes screen offers the same two filters, « Left: This week » and « Delivery: Not delivered », with their chips. The list of the notes takes `since`, an instant in milliseconds, and `undelivered=1`, which a guest is refused, and the read of the bar counts both.
